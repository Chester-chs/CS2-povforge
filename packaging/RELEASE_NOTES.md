## PovForge v0.15.10

Download **PovForge-Setup.exe** to install or update; **PovForge-windows.zip** is the portable edition.

### English

- Hide Perspective replays / Victim perspective in head-to-head mode.
- Ignore previously saved victim replay opt-ins for head-to-head tasks, while retaining the normal A/B views and the saved choices for other modes.

### 简体中文

下载安装或更新请使用 **PovForge-Setup.exe**；免安装版使用 **PovForge-windows.zip**。

- 双人对位数据中不再显示“视角重放／受害者视角”整块设置。
- 双人对位任务忽略之前保存的受害者视角开关，保留原有双方视角；其他模式仍保留原有选择。

No CS2 launch or changes to the owner's game settings were made during development. Style editing and AI API integration are unchanged.
开发时未启动 CS2，未修改用户游戏设置；本次不修改成片风格或接入 AI API。
