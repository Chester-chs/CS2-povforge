## PovForge v0.16.0

Download **PovForge-Setup.exe** to install or update; **PovForge-windows.zip** is the portable edition.

### English

- See estimated main-footage duration and merged segment count before recording. Processing-time and output-size ranges use at least three similar completed jobs on this PC.
- Open the new Video library to search by player, map or title, filter modes, play videos and open folders. Records survive restarts and queue clearing; existing output-folder videos are also listed.
- All five modes show per-round kill weapons. Expand for event times, attacker/victim and headshots. Head-to-head is limited to the selected pair.
- Old Demo caches need one additional read for weapon/timing details, then reuse them. The library does not reparse Demos.

### 简体中文

下载安装或更新请使用 **PovForge-Setup.exe**；免安装版使用 **PovForge-windows.zip**。

- 录制前查看主体画面预计时长与合并后的片段数。同类录制完成至少 3 次后，根据本机历史记录估算制作耗时与成片大小。
- 新增成片库：搜索选手、地图或视频名称，按模式筛选，播放视频或打开文件夹。重启和清空队列后仍保留记录，也可找到保存目录内的旧视频。
- 五种模式均显示每回合的击杀武器；展开可看事件时间、击杀双方、武器和爆头。双人对位只显示所选双方的交手。
- 旧 Demo 缓存首次补读武器与时间数据后继续复用。成片库不会重新解析 Demo。

Duration estimates exclude extra replays, intros/outros, slow motion and transitions. Old videos may have unknown metadata. No AI editor is included. Development and testing did not launch CS2 or change the owner's game settings.
预计时长不包含额外重放、片头片尾、慢动作和转场；旧视频的部分信息可能未知。本版不包含 AI 剪辑。开发和验证未启动 CS2，未修改用户游戏设置。
