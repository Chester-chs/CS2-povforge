**Windows app / Windows 程序**

1. Download `PovForge-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, double-click `PovForge.exe`.
   下载 `PovForge-windows.zip`，解压到任意位置，双击 `PovForge.exe`。
2. First time: click **Install HLAE and FFmpeg** (bottom left). You need Steam and CS2 installed.
   第一次使用：点左下角 **一键安装 HLAE 和 FFmpeg**。需要已安装 Steam 和 CS2。
3. Pick a demo, click a player, record. Quit FACEIT / 5E / Perfect World first.
   选 demo，点选手，开始录制。录制前先退出 FACEIT / 5E / 完美世界。

Recording starts CS2 with `-insecure` (VAC off for that session); your CS2 settings are backed up and restored.
录制时 CS2 以 `-insecure` 启动（该次不连 VAC 服务器）；你的 CS2 设置会备份并在结束后还原。
